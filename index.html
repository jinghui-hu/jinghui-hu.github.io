More about my background +

I’m a researcher in the School of Computing and Communications at Lancaster University, working with Hans Gellersen. My work spans gaze interaction, extended reality (XR), and multimodal interfaces.

I completed my PhD at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Per Ola Kristensson. I’m interested in connecting what we know about human sensorimotor behaviour with the design of interactive and intelligent systems.
